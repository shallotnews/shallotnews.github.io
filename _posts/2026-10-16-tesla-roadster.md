As of late, Elon Musk's Tesla has announced 
their next-generation "Roadster" vehicle, and the 
critics are already angry. The main selling point of the vehicle is its ability to 
fly via compressed gas boosters designed by SpaceX. With many, many 
add-ons, the Roadster can supposedly go from 0-95 km/h in just 1.1 
seconds. Musk also revealed the car can do tricks, such as a kickflip, and 
it can land from upside-down with ease. Some conspiracy theorists were 
claiming the car was attached to strings, and the giant clouds of gas were 
merely a disguise to allow the car to break free of them. One states "It's all 
a big sham. That thing could never fly, just like the moon landing. Do you 
ever wonder why the flag stood up straight on the moon?! Wake up, 
people!!" Critics also pointed out the ridiculous starting price, with the 
down payment of at least US$250 000, with prices expected to increase 
before the launch date of 2028 or later.
