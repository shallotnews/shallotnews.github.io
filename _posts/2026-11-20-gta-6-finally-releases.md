Gamers across the entire world were rejoicing over the fact that Rockstar Games released the next instalment in the Grand Theft Auto series, GTA VI. It has been over 13 years since the release of GTA V, and Thursday marked the largest video game release of all time. One big problem, in which Rockstar is facing a lot of backlash, is that every major reviewer called it "trash" and "dogshit". Many pointed out the numerous glitches, the game's price (US$100), and that it runs at 30 frames per second. Game review company IGN said in a statement to The Shallot on Friday stating "The beer just spawns out of nowhere. The detail on the 747 livery is inaccurate, and most of all, the road lines are off-colour! 2/10." Other independent reviews have also shut down the praise, with many stating they will protest in front of Rockstar headquarters in New York City. Gamers have also expressed their concern, as they are disappointed as some flew to New Zealand in order to play the game early. Rockstar Games has not commented on these remarks. Another topic of conversation surrounding the game is people asking for a day off or skipping school to play the game. Some have allowed them, and some have come back early due to how bad the game is. As more updates roll out, The Shallot will report.
