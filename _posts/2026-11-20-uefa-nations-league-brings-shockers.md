title: UEFA Nations League brings shockers across the board
date: 2026-11-20

The UEFA Nations League is a yearly international competition in Europe in which teams participate in groups, separated by "leagues" based on recent performance. This week saw some extremely shocking scorelines, such as Germany defeating the Netherlands 5-0 and England defeating Spain 3-2. Italy continues to not care about any international competition, as they are currently placed 3rd in their group with France, Belgium, and Türkiye. Local Meseker Issac states "Germany is winning it all. No shot they lose to Belgium quarterfinals."
