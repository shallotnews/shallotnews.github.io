---
title:  "School resorts to phone lockers to get students to pay attention"
date:   2026-11-6
---
![Source: schoolspecialty.com](https://cdn.schoolspecialty.com/ea3b3fb6-f3fa-4e71-bb73-b3800031ba58/2155649_A_JPG%20Output.jpg?width=700&height=700&fit=bounds&canvas=700,700&bg-color=ffffff)

On October 30, it was announced in the Blazer Press that, starting the 
week of November 2, students would have to put their phones in a locker 
at the start of every class period. The locker, pictured above, can hold 
around thirty phones and is unlocked via a non-reversible key 
mechanism. This decision immediately sparked outrage amongst 
students and parents, one junior claiming "Nah man, this is some BS. 
First we can't use our phones during class, and now we can't even have 
'em! At this point, just leave it at home, man." According to the Blazer 
Press, written by principal Damon Monteleone, the administration has 
implemented a "soft launch" for the first three days, allowing teachers to 
not fully enforce it yet. Some students have reported their teachers not 
giving a shit and letting them keep them in their pockets anyways. Not 
just students have expressed their concern, however. The main issue 
parents think they have with students not having their phones with them 
is they will not be able to contact them in the event of an emergency. 
Although the administration listened to the so-called whining, this did not 
affect their final decision. This change was made to comply with Maryland 
General Assembly bill No.630. The key detail left out by MCPS, who is 
trying to enforce the rule, is that the law is not even effective until the 
2027-28 school year, let alone in a need to be enforced. As this story 
develops, The Shallot will report on the current state of the situation.
