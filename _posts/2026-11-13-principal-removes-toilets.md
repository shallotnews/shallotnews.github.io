Despite backlash from students and the community, Montgomery Blair High School principal Damon Monteleone made the decision Wednesday to remove all toilets from the school bathrooms. In a statement released by the administration on Thursday, the decision was made out of "an abundance of caution" due to increased gang activity in the bathrooms. One teacher said to The Shallot, "If they can't use the bathroom, it ain't an excuse." This decision has been controversial among the student body and parents, with some voicing their concern on how students will excrete waste during the school day. One parent reports "Are they just supposed to shit in the porta-potties? I tell you, this damn school. Shoulda switched to socialism."
