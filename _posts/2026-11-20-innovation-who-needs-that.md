Every other Wednesday, students at Montgomery Blair High School in Silver Spring, Maryland dread going to school, like every day. What makes this day special is the "Innovation" period where one of the four daily classes is extended by 45 minutes. Students have voiced their concerns in the past, with one reporting "Innovation is dumb as shit. I don't think there's any innovating going on in the school, especially with what they're feeding us at lunch." Others have complained that their teachers do not even participate in the mandatory "lesson plan" which is often AI generated. This, along with Infoflow, is regarded by teachers as another part of the bullshit they have to put up with, or they get faced by David Monteleone, nicknamed "the career destroyer". Alternatives to this schedule change have been presented, especially with the shift to a 7-period schedule next school year. Some ideas proposed by students include cancelling school every Wednesday, 19 weeks of winter break, and impeaching Principal Monteleone. "We're supposed to be a democracy, right? Then how about we impeach this bum? This is the real cost of socialism in the US." As the schedule probably continues to change, The Shallot will be monitoring any unexpected developments.
