title: “It was doomed from the start”, critics say on Homecoming game and dance
date: 2026-10-16
---
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shallotnews/shallotnews.github.io/b24f9b75495a70309667fe52812f99a234c0ec04/assets/images/IMG_0310%20(1).JPEG)
Blazers were excited to come back to school for the second time last week Friday for the homecoming football game against Walt Whitman High 
School of Bethesda. Students reported the vibe of the game to be 
amazing, and the game started off well, with Blair taking a 16 point lead 
by halftime. One of the highlights of the game was when face paint was 
passed around, with many students lathering their faces with it, 
resembling Norwegian football fans. Freshman Ezra Stern reported to The 
Shallot stating "the game was historic, for the first time ever, the magnet 
kids left the computer lab to look at the stadium lights. I think they 
thought they were an ultra rare quantum phenomenon or something." 
However, after halftime, Whitman slowly made a comeback, completing 
the remontada 29-26. After the home crowd rested disappointed, many people returned the next night for the homecoming dance, expecting world-class food for a ticket worth US$22. "How the hell does it cost 22 dollars to give us some damn pizza?!" one attendee reported. Other students expressed concern, stating "I spilled a drink on my shirt during the dance and I had to go to the 
SweetFrog bathroom to check how bad it was, 
because there were no fucking mirrors within 
100m of the school." Some criticised the DJ for 
playing terrible songs, while others claimed the 
things the school had to offer were "lame and 
pathetic." The SGA advertised the events as a 
"huge success" and showcased some of the 
photos taken at the venue.
