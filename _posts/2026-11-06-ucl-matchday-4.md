Matchday 4 of the UEFA Champions League promised some good
fixtures, but the scorelines proved they were more than "just a shock". To
start, on Wednesday, PSG beat Villareal 6-0 away, with PSG scoring four
goals in 17 minutes. Manchester United were down to Roma 0-2 at home
at halftime, but then Roma quickly conceded a retaken penalty, followed
by Cunha scoring a brace to finally put United with at least one win
before the end of the season. A similar story happened with supposedly
world-class team Real Madrid, with them narrowly beating A.E.K. Athens
3-2 away. However, the largest defeat was yet to come, with Como beating
Lens 9-1 away, the largest away win in UCL history. Fans were overjoyed.
Football commentary creator Guapmotion states "that's not a belt-to-ass,
just REMOVE THE ENTIRE CLUB!!" Fenerbahce also beat Liverpool 3-2 at
home, with fans including local Rahm Issacman outraged by their loss.
Matchday 5 is set for November 24/25, so tune into The Shallot for the
special Thanksgiving article!
