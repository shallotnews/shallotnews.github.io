As the homecoming game was rapidly approaching in the late weeks of 
September, students at Montgomery Blair High School in Maryland 
were shocked to hear that the recent hikes in petrol and diesel prices 
have even affected their community. One of the most important sporting 
events of the year, homecoming, is rapidly approaching, and word spread 
that tickets would be priced at $20 for the cheapest tier. "Twenty dollars?! 
I can buy like, 4 big macs for that." freshman Rian Faye Dowd stated 
Friday. According to [mbhs.edu](mbhs.edu), the price tiers (excl. VAT) go as follows: 
Basic: entry, with ads: $20, Premium: entry, with no ads: $40, Premium 
Plus: Premium, with a free drink: $50. The website also states a $25 seat reservation is required to sit down. This clearly 
sparked outrage within the student body, with 
violent right-wing extremists flooding the halls. 
No talks have gone underway on reducing the 
price.
