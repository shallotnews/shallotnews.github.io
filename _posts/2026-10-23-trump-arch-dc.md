title: Trump to build freedom arch near DC, old people outraged
date: 2026-10-23
---
![Source: Duane Lempke via Wikipedia](<../assets/images/ArlingtonMemorialBridgeEast.jpg>)
Plans for the construction of the "Triumphal Arch" were announced by Donald Trump in December of 2025, but these unrealistic dreams have inched closer and closer to reality, and as of late, it was revealed that construction may begin as soon as November 5, 2026. Initial plans for the arch described it as "finished by 2060", just like any American infrastructure project. The arch, should it be built, will be located in Arlington County, 3km (1.8mi) north of Reagan Nat'l Airport and 500m (1500 ft) from Arlington Cemetery metro station. Long-time Arlington County veteran Paul Sansen states "This doggone arch is just a big sham. When I'm in that cemetery, I don't want no big arch gettin' in the way of my tombstone!" Veterans like Sansen have expressed their concern, with some condemning the arch's resemblance to the Arc de Triomphe in Paris. Not just veterans are mad, as one Japanese tourist reported to The Shallot, "This is worse than Sendai. Now there's a big arch taking all of our attention from the sakura. I better just go back now." Whether or not the construction will begin is up to the commission, who, according to house speaker Mike Johnson, better "get their asses off their swivel chairs or get sent to Guantanamo Bay."
