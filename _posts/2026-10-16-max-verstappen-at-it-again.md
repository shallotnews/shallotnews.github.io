The world of Formula one opened its eyes once again last week at the 
aptly named Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia, as after a season full of 
disappointing finishes and mechanical issues, Max Verstappen finally 
remembered how to win again. “It’s all in the mindset” said Verstappen, 
adding that “you need to concentrate fully on how much the other drivers 
are pissing you off.” Max’s father, Jos Verstappen, added; “It’s good to 
have Max winning again. My whipping arm is starting to hurt”. This grand prix was a highly unusual one, as it was first 
of all named for a country it was not in. 
Because of the war in Iran, all of the Middle 
East races had been cancelled except the 
Bahrain grand prix, whose executives 
simply decided to invade Malaysia instead. 
After losing the lead at the start of the race, 
Verstappen won it back 2 laps later by 
triggering the preinstalled self destruct 
button of the opposing driver, George 
Russell of Mercedes. The Shallot will

expected motorsports events.
